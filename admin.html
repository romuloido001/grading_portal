Learners
Import many learners at once
One learner per line: LRN ; Name ; Age ; Sex ; Section (you can also paste rows copied from Excel/Google Sheets). LRN must be 12 digits. Existing LRNs are skipped. A 6-digit parent access code is made for each learner.

Subjects
Add, rename, reorder or remove subjects. Choose "Part of" to make a subject a component of another (e.g. Music and Arts → MAPEH); the parent's grade is then the average of its components and isn't typed in. Renaming keeps existing grades. Removing a subject deletes its grades and teacher assignments.

Report card logos
